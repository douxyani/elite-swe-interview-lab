## Additional first-party preparation evidence

Jane Street explicitly says LeetCode/HackerRank are useful algorithmic practice but not exact replicas of its interviews, emphasizes communication and clear/correct code, and publishes retired interviews: https://www.janestreet.com/mock-interview/ . Its official preparation page also states SWE interviews are programming-focused rather than mental-math/logic-puzzle screens: https://www.janestreet.com/preparing-for-a-software-engineering-interview/ .

G-Research's official interview article says the refactoring stage is meant to resemble real-world software work, with a working IDE and internet access; its design stage probes trade-offs and business-level questions; algorithm questions remain part of the process: https://www.gresearch.com/news/what-to-expect-from-a-quant-platform-software-engineering-interview/ .

OpenAI says “high potential” includes ramping quickly in a new domain and producing results; its skills assessment format can vary by team: https://openai.com/interview-guide/ . Anthropic says its technical interviews use live coding tools such as Colab and CodeSignal: https://www.anthropic.com/careers .
