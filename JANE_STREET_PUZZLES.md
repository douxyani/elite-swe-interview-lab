# Jane Street Puzzles: useful, but separate from SWE interview coding

Jane Street's official SWE-preparation page says SWE interviews are programming-focused and do not use mental-math or logic-puzzle questions. Do **not** replace coding preparation with puzzles: https://www.janestreet.com/preparing-for-a-software-engineering-interview/ .

Still, Jane Street itself says creative problem solving/puzzling is intrinsic to its work, and it maintains a large official archive: https://www.janestreet.com/puzzles/ and https://www.janestreet.com/puzzles/archive/ . These are good “unfamiliar problem” cross-training.

Suggested picks from the official archive, chosen to vary representation rather than because they are SWE interview questions:

- **Robot Capture-the-Flag** (April 2024) — algorithmic state/reasoning flavor.
- **Robot Long Jump** (March 2023) — optimization/search flavor.
- **Robot Road Trip** (July 2025) — route/state reasoning.
- **Hooks** series — constraint propagation and representation.
- **Knight Moves** series — graph-like spatial constraints.
- **Number Cross** series — dense constraint interaction.
- **Tree-edge Triage** (August 2024) — useful if you enjoy structural/graph reasoning.
- **Current archive puzzles** — use a recent one cold rather than reading solutions.

When you solve one, write a short postmortem: the representation that unlocked it, the dead-end assumption, and the smallest hint that would have redirected you. That postmortem is more valuable for interview transfer than the final answer alone.
